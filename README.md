# A Critique of the Political Economy of Human Civilization — The Civilization Asset Audit Report and the Five Treasures

**作者 / Author: Feng 12（锋十二）**

## 中文

本仓库收录"五件法宝"全文，按阅读顺序排列：

1. **入口** ——《女性如何被一层层吃掉》：道德审判是人类史上最大的屠杀。九层吃法，层层有账。
2. **准则** ——《文明资产审计报告》：序 + 三十六章 + 终页。对人类文明一万年资产负债表的总审计：交易所、准备金、银行、债务、清算、古典政治经济学盲区、拆桥。（因单文件体积限制，全文按篇分为六个分卷文件，存放在 `zh/02-准则·文明资产审计报告/` 目录内，按文件名顺序连读即为全本。）
3. **判例** ——《局外人》：一个案件的完整庭审记录。（仅中文版）
4. **出口** ——《止血玩具·蜜蜂和狼》：清算完成之后，生活在这层。
5. **经** ——《梦里的乞丐睡着的皇帝·知道》：观测即乞丐，证明即地狱。本质先于存在。

发行规则：每一语种，每十万使用人口，限发一册纸质版。电子版免费流通。

## English

This repository holds the complete texts of the **Five Treasures**, in reading order:

1. **The Entrance** — *How a Woman Gets Eaten, Layer by Layer*: moral judgment is the largest massacre in human history. Nine layers, each with its own ledger.
2. **The Standard** — *The Civilization Asset Audit Report*: preface, thirty-six chapters, and a final page. A full audit of ten thousand years of human civilization's balance sheet: the Exchange, the Reserve, the Banks, the Debt, the Liquidation, the blind spot of classical political economy, and the burning of the bridges. (For file-size reasons the full text is split into per-book files inside `en/02-The-Standard-The-Civilization-Asset-Audit-Report/`; read them in filename order.)
3. **The Case** — *The Outsider*: a complete trial record. (Chinese edition only, by the author's decision.)
4. **The Exit** — *The Styptic Toy: The Bee and the Wolf*: after the liquidation, life lives on this floor.
5. **The Sutra** — *The Beggar in the Dream, the Emperor Asleep · To Know*: to observe is to become a beggar; to prove is to enter hell. Essence precedes existence.

Issuance rule: for each language, one printed copy per hundred thousand speakers. The electronic edition circulates free of charge.
