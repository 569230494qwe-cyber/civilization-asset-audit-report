# Book III: The Banks — The Harvesting System of Derivative Currencies

## Chapter 14: The Traditional Bank — Duty as the Absolute Standard

The first derivative currency — we begin the dissection at the oldest counter.

The Traditional Bank is the first nesting doll issued by the Patriarchal Narrative Central Bank, and the oldest branch. It has only one core product: duty. Its slogan has only one sentence: "As long as you keep your duty, you have value."

What is duty? It is obedience. Obedience to parents, obedience to the husband, obedience to the clan. Do not live for yourself, do not live for your own desires, do not live for your own ambition. The entire meaning of your existence is to be a good daughter, a good wife, a good mother.

Its reserve is the most primitive right of ownership over the female body. You are handed from your father's hands into your husband's hands; your name changes from "so-and-so's daughter" to "so-and-so's wife," and then to "so-and-so's mother." You yourself are never an independent being; you are forever attached to a grander narrative.

The handover procedure is called a wedding. The transfer fee is called a bride price. The most ceremonious day of your life is, in essence, a collateral transfer ceremony — only the emcee will not read the lines this way.

You spend a lifetime redeeming the Duty Coin, and the bank promises you can exchange it for the title of "good woman." But the problem is: the redemption window is never in your hands.

Your parents-in-law can judge you "unfilial"; your husband can judge you "disobedient." You can never prove that you are "dutiful" enough, because the creditor will never let you settle the debt.

Note the exquisite design in the contract terms of the Duty Coin: it has no entry for "paid off." A mortgage has a day when it is paid off; duty does not. When you reach ninety points, the standard rises to ninety-five; when you reach ninety-nine, the missing point is "someone else's daughter-in-law." The interest rolls forever; the principal is sealed forever.

So the Duty Coin can never truly be cashed. While you live, you are a debt; after you die, you are a written-off bad loan.

The Duty Coin is the initial stock issued by the Patriarchal Bank. But in the course of modernization, it suffered severe inflation: when women discovered that what "keeping their duty" bought them was only silent exploitation, this coin began to devalue madly — devalued into a Zimbabwean note with a face value of ten billion.

The old currency collapsed. But a bank never sits and waits for death — the reserve is still in its hands; it need only issue a new coin, change the name, change the sales pitch, and the women lining up at the counter will once again wind into a circle.

The new coin's name is also very familiar to you.

It is called "independence."

## Chapter 15: The Independence Bank — The Busy-Bee Nesting Dolls

The new coin's name is "independence."

The Duty Coin had devalued, so the Patriarchal Bank immediately rolled out its second nesting doll — the Independence Bank. Its core slogan is: "You do not rely on men; you rely on yourself."

The independent woman — the term looks like a crown offered to women. But take it apart, and you will find countless Russian dolls nested inside.

The first word is "dignity." It says: you must be independent to have dignity.
Then comes "diligence." It says: you must work hard to prove your value.
Then "bravery." It says: you must dare to fight, dare to venture, dare to carry everything alone.
Then "strength." It says: you cannot cry, cannot be weak, cannot depend, cannot break down.
Then "emotional stability." It says: you must be mature, rational, sensible; do not trouble anyone.
Then "balance career and family." It says: you must be a workplace elite, and at the same time a good mother.
Then "answer emails three days after giving birth." It says: this is what a truly independent woman is.

Does this vocabulary of praise sound familiar?

Yes. It is humanity's millennia-old praise of the little bee.

The Independence Bank packaged the entirety of humanity's praise of the bee and sold it to women: you are an independent woman; like the diligent little bee, you press forward bravely, relying on yourself. This sales pitch sounds incomparably grand, incomparably correct.

But the truth is: the bee is praised not because it gathers honey, but because not one drop of the honey it gathers belongs to it.

The bee gathers honey all its life, and not a drop in the hive belongs to it. It is merely the producer, not the owner. Its "diligence" is written into poems, its "selfless dedication" is woven into fairy tales, the "little bee spirit" is made into slogans. And the honey is taken away.

What the Independence Bank does to women is exactly the same.

It praises your independence, praises your strength, praises your self-reliance. But follow the flow of the honey and take a look: the honey you gather by day at the company goes into the boss's reports; the honey you gather by night at home goes into the family's dishes. Gathering at both ends, and neither end belongs to you. The honey flows in a thousand different directions, with one common point: it does not belong to the one who gathered it.

And all your effort, all your vitality, all your "independence" is ultimately exchanged not for freedom, but for a Zimbabwean note with a face value of ten billion.

Even more exquisite is this coin's redemption clause: your independence must be continuously proven to external standards. It must be seen by the boss, recognized by colleagues, acknowledged by society, liked on social media. The more independent you are, the more thoroughly you are observed; the more independent you are, the more inseparable you become from the system that issues you your evaluations.

An independence that can only hold when watched, an autonomy that can only be confirmed when liked — is it freedom, or another form of clocking in?

The Duty Coin ties you to the home. The Independence Coin ties you to the KPI. The rope changed color; the other end of the rope is still held in the same bank's hand.

"Having gathered honey from a hundred flowers — for whom does she toil, and for whom is it sweet?"

And when the coin "independence" also begins to overdraw its credit — when more and more women discover that the end of the little bee is still the hive — the bank rolls out the third nesting doll. This time, it stops talking about ideals.

It talks only about money.

## Chapter 16: The Big-Results Bank — Monetization as the Absolute Standard

The third nesting doll does not talk about ideals. It talks only about money.

When the Independence Coin began to overdraw its credit, the Patriarchal Bank immediately threw out the Big-Results Bank. Its logic is extremely candid: it does not speak to you of self-actualization, does not speak of independence and autonomy; it says one thing only — get big results.

What are big results? Wealth, class ascension, a house, a car, a hefty bride price. It no longer demands that you toil bitterly as a little bee; it tells you directly: the greatest success of a woman's life is measured by how big a result she can take home.

This is the most deceptive of the four banks, precisely because of its candor. The Traditional Bank speaks of sentiment, the Independence Bank speaks of ideals, and the Big-Results Bank slaps the ledger directly on the table — so it appears especially "real," especially "clear-eyed about the world."

But candor is not freedom. A person doing life in prison can also say, "I see things clearly." Seeing clearly that you are in prison, and walking out of the prison, are two different things.

The reserve of the Big-Results Coin is the Chastity Coin in its most naked form: body, youth, beauty, fertility — all packaged into standardized assets. The previous two banks at least wrapped the collateral in a layer of narrative wrapping paper; this one lists it on the market stark naked.

And its cruelest feature lies not in teaching women to monetize, but in introducing an extremely brutal mechanism: female rivalry.

It erects benchmarks in the market: "Look at that woman — married well, got big results." "Look at her — a bride price of millions, financially free." And so all women are swept into a smokeless arms race: you're pretty, so I must get plastic surgery; your figure is good, so I must get liposuction; you know how to provide emotional value, so I enroll in a "socialite training class"; you got a bride price of five hundred thousand, so I must demand a million.

Women fight until their heads bleed, exhausting their youth and vitality, trampling one another, demeaning one another, out-competing one another. And the Patriarchal Bank sits high above, watching this race it started itself, harvesting all the contestants together with Zimbabwean notes of ten-billion denomination.

This is the Big-Results Bank: it is the first to speak the Chastity Coin's collateral nature out loud, and the posture of speaking it aloud makes you mistake it for a comrade helping you escape — but the one who points out the prison is also part of the prison.

And when monetization's name also stinks, when the word "gold-digger" begins to circulate in the market, when more and more women discover that the end of this road is universal condemnation — the bank rolls out the fourth nesting doll.

This time it talks neither of money nor of ideals.

It talks of dreams.

## Chapter 17: The Cinderella Bank and the Prince-Charming Coin — The Ultimate Currency Jointly Guaranteed by Four Banks

The three banks above each have their own kind of exhaustion. The Traditional Bank makes you keep your duty, the Independence Bank makes you a little bee, the Big-Results Bank makes you monetize. Is there a way to redeem all the benefits at once, without being so tired?

Yes. The Patriarchal Bank threw out the fourth nesting doll — the Cinderella Bank.

Its core promise: you do not need to strive, do not need to prove, do not need to monetize. You only need to wait. Wait for the right person to appear; wait for him to rescue you from your mediocre life.

But the Cinderella Coin has a fatal flaw: it is too old. A European fairy tale several hundred years old has a limited credit line; it cannot fool modern women who have seen much of the world. Old currency needs new packaging.

And so the Cinderella Bank performed a classic operation in monetary history: old wine in a new bottle — issuing the upgraded version, the Prince-Charming Coin.

Look at the structure of this coin — it is a masterpiece of currency engineering: issued by the Cinderella Bank, jointly guaranteed by the other three banks. One coin, four guarantors, simultaneously redeeming four promises:

He is rich and can monetize you in an instant — a clause guaranteed by the Big-Results Bank.
He is stronger than you, so you no longer need to be a little bee — a clause guaranteed by the Independence Bank.
He loves you and you alone, and rescues you from mediocrity — the Cinderella Bank's own coin.
He will eventually propose to you and give you a respectable family — a clause guaranteed by the Traditional Bank.

One coin, simultaneously redeeming the four promises of "money, strength, love, family." This is why its face value can be printed at one hundred trillion: it concentrates the entire credit of four banks; it is the highest-leverage currency in the history of human narrative.

But the truth of the matter goes far beyond this.

The Prince Charming is the board of directors of the Patriarchal Bank itself.

This is no romantic fairy tale at all; it is a naked class spectacle. The board are the ones who issue the currency; they do not need to use money to buy anything at all — because money itself is what they print. The issuer never needs to prove his purchasing power to the market.

What are bottom-tier men? The most miserable chives in the market. They have no issuing power; they can only sell their youth and sweat to exchange for a few counterfeit notes, and then try to use those notes to exchange for a partner's favor. Having poured in everything they have, what they buy is merely a "Prince-Charming experience card."

And women? They are likewise strangled inside this narrative matrix. The Cinderella Coin and the Prince-Charming Coin make them expect to "be chosen" — in essence, to expect to be selected by the board as a premium prize or an accessory asset. Even an A-list actress's credit rating can be shorted overnight; how much safety margin an ordinary woman's account has in this market — do the math yourself.

And the ones who finally catch all the resentment are the bottom-tier men.

The board is never afraid of women throwing feminist punches, and cares nothing for whether bottom-tier men live or die. Because it has completed a perfect narrative substitution: it converted a class question into a gender question. It fears only one thing — that women and bottom-tier men will tear off the invisibility cloaks of all these nesting dolls.

These four nesting dolls run simultaneously in reality, tangled into one another. Women hop back and forth among "duty, independence, big results, Cinderella," eaten dry in self-tearing; bottom-tier men desperately prove themselves worthy of a "CEO-type" label, emptying six wallets. And the board stays hidden, keeps reaping the profit.

This is the Chastity Coin's most perfect closed loop at the production stage: issuance, guarantee, circulation, harvest — all completed within a single system, leaving only customers attacking one another on the field.

In the next chapter, we look at what kind of man-eating monster these four banks form when put together.

It has four heads.

## Chapter 18: The Four-Headed Snake — The Competition and Automatic Harvesting of the Four Banks

**1. The Same Owner**

The Traditional Bank, the Independence Bank, the Big-Results Bank, the Cinderella Bank — on the surface they are four ways of living; in fact they issue one and the same currency — the Chastity Coin. They have merely printed four different pictures on the same coin.

Four banks, four sales pitches; each one attacks the other three, and each one tells women: don't go to the other three — what they hold is shit; only our coin is real.

But the four banks are owned by the same boss. Whichever one she chooses, what she mortgages is the same thing; whichever one she borrows from, the debt she owes is the same debt.

**2. The True Function of Competition**

The four banks compete with one another, ostensibly to fight for customers. But see through the ledger of this competition: the beneficiary of the competition is never any branch — it is the head office.

The ultimate purpose of female rivalry is not to let some one woman win. It is to make all women believe they are competing, improving, choosing — while they tear at one another, they think their opponents are other women, when in fact the true opponent is that invisible issuing power. The more they compete, the safer the bank; the more they tear at each other, the harder the Chastity Coin stands.

Undercutting each other on appearance, judging each other in narrative, bickering each other up in marriage and romance — three battlefields, all fighting the same war: women guarding women on behalf of the Patriarchal Narrative Bank.

**3. Four Heads, One Body**

This structure has a more accurate form: the four-headed snake.

One body, four heads, each responsible for one harvesting path, all pointing at the same reserve.

The first head bites "duty," harvesting traditional women — who provide the most basic collateral with their bodies and labor.
The second head bites "independence," harvesting career women — exchanging youth and health for a dignity that can never be redeemed.
The third head bites "monetization," harvesting women who want a shortcut — letting them consume one another in rivalry.
The fourth head bites "true love," harvesting women still dreaming — letting them exhaust their options in waiting.

The four heads tear at one another, negate one another, undermine one another. Each head is shouting: don't go to the other heads. But their body is one — all interest flows into the same vault; all debts are booked in the same ledger.

**4. Automatic Harvesting**

The most terrifying thing about this structure is that it needs no external drive. It runs by itself.

An ordinary day of a modern woman: in the morning, pressured to marry by her parents with the Duty Coin until she breaks down; in the afternoon, whipped by the workplace with the Busy-Bee Coin until she is drained; in the evening, stimulated by social media with the Big-Results Coin until she cannot sleep; late at night, hypnotized by the Prince-Charming Coin through a romantic drama.

Today she is bitten by the first head, tomorrow by the second. She thinks she is changing banks; in fact she is merely being swallowed from one head of the snake into another. Yesterday she agreed to a family-arranged blind date, today she attends a meeting in professional dress, tomorrow she goes to the gym to check in and post photos, the day after she signs up for a socialite training class. She splits herself into four people, working for four banks at the same time.

The harder she chooses, the more debt she has; the more anxious, the greater the issuance.

The four-headed snake does not need to win. It only needs you forever choosing among four heads. This is the four-headed snake's cruelest point: it disguises a man-eating structure as the illusion of free choice. You think you are thinking; in fact you are only being thought by four sets of grammar. You think you are choosing; in fact you are only being eaten by four multiple-choice questions. The modern woman, in countless "choices" for which she alone is made responsible, in countless options of "I do," smiles and feeds herself into the meat grinder.

Even Cao Xueqin would shake his head; he would surely write a new judgment verse for the modern woman:

And the debts bitten out by this snake do not disappear. They flow out of every bitten person and continue downstream along the river channels — into the family, into the workplace, into the next generation.

# Book IV: The Debt — To Hold Others in Contempt Is a Karmic Debt beyond Measure

## Chapter 19: The Debt Chain

**1. Making Debt**

Every debt begins with an act of contempt.

A glance, a sentence, an interruption, a turned back. "Who do you think you are?" "You?" "What do women know." "Scumbag." "Loser." — The gesture is small, so small the initiator himself cannot remember it.

But the ledger remembers.

The instant the contempt occurs, a debt is created: the person held in contempt has a sum deducted from his sense-of-existence account. That sum does not disappear. It only looks for an exit.

**2. Compound Interest**

The interest on the debt is calculated like this: the person who was deducted must recover an equal amount of sense of existence for the account to balance.

How? Two roads. Upward — demand repayment from the one who held him in contempt. But that is usually unattainable, because the contempt-holder is stronger than you. So only the second road remains: downward — find an account weaker than your own, deduct from it, and patch your own hole.

He was cursed by his boss, so he goes home and curses his wife. The wife was cursed, so she turns and curses the child. The child was cursed, so at school he bullies a smaller classmate. The smaller classmate goes home and kicks the cat.

Every transfer adds a sum of interest: the person to whom it is transferred must not only recover what was deducted from him, but also pass along the humiliation produced in the process. And so debt rolls onto debt, interest onto interest; with each additional link in the chain, the total grows by one more circle.

Contempt is the seigniorage of this machine. It requires no central bank meeting, no resolution of any kind — every mouth has issuing power; and in the end it turns into poison and returns to everyone's stomach.

**3. Networking: A Bank without a Board of Directors**

A single act of contempt is not terrible; what is terrible is that it networks itself automatically.

The four banks at least still have signboards; the debt chain does not even have a signboard. It hides in the everyday, circulating under the cloaks of "education," "concern," "just joking," "it's for your own good." Debt flows along social relations: rank, seniority, gender, intimacy — wherever there is a height difference, there is a riverbed of debt. It flows from upstream to downstream, from strong accounts to weak accounts, from adults to children, and is then pressed into the food chain — pressed from the top layer by layer, until not a single link on the whole chain is clean.

This net has no center. There is no "creditors' office" managing it. Everyone is a debtor; everyone is a creditor; everyone is deducted by the link above and deducts from the link below.

You are simultaneously on dozens of chains. You are downstream of your boss, downstream of your landlord, downstream of every trending topic; and you are also upstream of the delivery rider, upstream of the customer-service agent, upstream of every "person who has nothing to do with you." This net has no designer, no board, no general accounting office. It does not hate you. It simply runs.

This book judges the knife, not the person. What is to be liquidated is the machine, not any single gear in it — every gear is, at the same time, being crushed by other gears.

But if you have only heard this far, you may feel that the debt chain is, after all, an abstract thing — a conceptual net.

Wrong. It is physical. It is your stomach.

**4. Materialization: The Debt Chain Is Settled in Your Stomach**

At the CCTV 3·15 Gala in 2022, the pickled-cabbage suppliers of several famous instant-noodle brands were exposed: the cabbage was pickled in earthen pits, workers trampled it barefoot, flicked their finished cigarette butts into the pit, and preservative levels exceeded the standard by up to ten times. The same company used standardized pickling pools for export cabbage and earthen pits for the domestic market. Why two sets? Because the export cabbage has to face other people's customs, while the domestic cabbage only has to face you — in their ledger, you do not exist.

In July 2024, Beijing News found in an investigation: tanker trucks that had hauled coal-to-oil chemicals were loaded directly with edible soybean oil without the tanks being cleaned. Chemical residues were mixed into the oil bottles of thousands upon thousands of households. One sentence in the report deserves to be copied onto the wall: it was an "open secret" in the industry.

In August 2026, a disinfection company in Xiamen was found to have long used dichlorvos to "disinfect" dozens of chain restaurants. To dodge inspections, employees poured dichlorvos concentrate into mineral-water bottles with the labels torn off and sprayed it directly onto tableware and furniture, with ingredients sitting right beside them. And one company employee said a sentence that may serve as this chapter's footnote:

"Restaurants we've disinfected — I personally would never eat at them. Not even if you beat me to death."

And there are the vegetable vendors who dip baby cabbage in formaldehyde to keep it fresh — in Liaocheng, Shandong, a profit of one hundred eleven yuan, fined one hundred twenty thousand; this "craft" has persisted from 2012 to 2026 — fourteen years; the place changed, the people changed, the technique did not.

This is not the story of one black-hearted company. It is the same technique, growing year after year within an industry. Which proves one thing: the debt chain does not need bad people. There are no devils on the chain — only nodes.

Now, put these news items side by side, and you will see the complete circuit of the debt chain:

The cabbage makers know they threw in cigarette butts, so they do not eat pickled cabbage — but they need oil to stir-fry. The oil haulers know what remains in the tank, so they buy small bottles of imported oil for themselves — but they have to eat at restaurants. The disinfection worker "would not eat even if beaten to death" at the restaurants he disinfected — but he has to eat; every takeout order he places may come from another person who "would not eat it even if beaten to death."

Every person precisely sidesteps the debt he himself made. Every debt makes one full circle and returns, with principal and interest, into another person's bowl. And that bowl, one turn later, returns to your table.

Reading to this point, put your anger away properly: aim it at the machine, not at the nodes. The workers in the cabbage pit, the disinfection workers pouring dichlorvos — they too are only downstream links on the debt chain, holding Poverty Coins, held in contempt by the whole of society; they are merely "outsourced labor," "dispatched workers." To hate them is to issue one more new debt.

## Chapter 20: Intergenerational Transmission

At the end of the last chapter it was said that besides horizontal transmission, the debt chain has a vertical trunk line: from one generation to the next.

**1. Born into a Position**

A child is born holding not a single currency. He is the poorest person in this Exchange — no savings, no credit record; even his name is issued by someone else.

But on his first day of birth he is already holding a position. What he holds is his parents' ledger.

IOUs of the sense of existence can be inherited. This is the debt chain's most unfair rule, and its most stable one: the confirmation the father had deducted outside is deducted back from the son at home; the mother whose valuation was pressed down all her life turns and presses down her daughter's valuation. The child has done nothing yet, and is already a debtor.

**2. The Debtor Who Cannot Resist**

The iron rule of downward deduction is to always pick the target that cannot resist. In a family, who can least resist?

The child.

A child is fully open-positioned to his parents' language — whatever the parents say, he deposits; he does not audit, does not hedge, sets no stop-loss.

So the sentence "why are you so stupid" is, to an adult, one deduction — but to a child it is an issuance: he accepts the sentence as the anchor of his self-valuation, and every day for the rest of his life repeats it to himself in his heart. The parents no longer need to open their mouths — the debtor himself has taken over the collections.

This is the cruelest operation of the distillation mechanism: one sentence from the parents is distilled by the child into an inner voice; the inner voice is further distilled into personality; the personality is further distilled into destiny. Three distillations, and all the losses are borne by the child.

The debt chain's ultimate compound interest is right here: the debtor grows up by himself, and goes on deducting from himself.

Every child who grows up on a debt chain is the most diligent market-watcher in that family.

He stares at his parents' faces the way a trader stares at the market: Dad's footsteps sound wrong today; Mom's silence is the precursor of a fall. At a tender age he has already mastered a technique many adults have not — tape reading. Because his physical safety and his existential valuation are both listed on those two K-lines.

**3. Two Currencies, a Dual-Currency Position**

Intergenerational transmission is not one blurred mass of influence. It has a precise division of currency types.

The son inherits the dignity treadmill. No crying, no cowering, no losing face; you must make something of yourself. "How many points did the other family's child score?" — the function of this sentence is not education but quotation: pricing one's own child at the external exchange rate. From the day he can understand speech, he is standing on the treadmill.

What the daughter inherits is two things.

The first is the Chastity Coin ledger. Bookkeeping begins in childhood: be good, be quiet and proper, don't give outsiders anything to laugh at; a girl must act like a girl. Every sentence is an accrual — her credit record, from the day she can understand speech, begins to be jointly maintained by the whole family.

The second is the treadmill. Note: this is a new configuration that only this generation has. The previous generation of girls had only the Chastity Coin in their ledgers; in this generation's ledgers, not one page of the Chastity Coin's clauses has been removed, and a Dignity Coin treadmill has been mounted on top — she too must score first place, must attend a famous school, must distinguish herself, must "make something of herself." And so she must run fast, yet not be "unlike a girl"; must distinguish herself, yet not be "too strong"; must win, yet be good.

The boy's treadmill only requires running. The girl's treadmill must be run while carrying the ledger.

The same machine levies one tax on the son and two taxes on the daughter. And then the adults look at this doubly deducted child and praise her: what a sensible child.

**4. "For Your Own Good": The Wrapping Paper of Debt**

Reading to this point, the guardrail must be stated once more: parents are not the enemy.

They are the previous relay runners, and what they hold in their hands is the same baton handed down from their own parents — only the baton is made of debt. They do not know what it is they are passing on. They genuinely mean well.

"It's all for your own good." — This sentence is true.

But note: a true debt is still a debt. Wrapping paper does not change the amount on the IOU. A machine that has run for ten thousand years does not need any link to harbor malice; it only needs every link to feel that it has done nothing wrong.

The chain of debt cannot be broken in a hundred million years. Because it is not transmitted by hatred — it is transmitted by love.

Hatred puts people on alert; love does not. A child can guard against a stranger's malice, but not against his mother's catchphrases. Debt is mixed into love and fed drop by drop — fed for twenty years, fed into the bones, fed into his personality, his tastes, his eye for choosing a spouse, the tone in which he educates his own children.

This is the true reason this machine has not broken down in ten thousand years: not because it is sturdy, but because every generation oils it with the most precious thing in its life — love.
