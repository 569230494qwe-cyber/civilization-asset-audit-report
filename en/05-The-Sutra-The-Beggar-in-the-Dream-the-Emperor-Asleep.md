# The Beggar in the Dream, the Emperor Asleep

Once there was an Emperor, who dreamed he was a beggar.

The beggar worked very hard — at dreaming himself an Emperor.

Every morning he woke to find himself in rags, living in a ruined temple, begging for food, filthy, stinking, laughed at by everyone.

So he began, desperately, to prove that he was the Emperor:

With the coldest logic, the beggar negated the will, arguing "the beggar is mere appearance — inwardly I am the Emperor who negates the will";

With the fiercest will, the beggar affirmed eternal recurrence, crying "I love fate — I am the Superman Emperor";

With the sharpest scalpel, the beggar dissected "hell is other people," proving himself the Emperor and all others beggars in need of his salvation.

The beggar kept mistaking costumed performers in dragon robes for the true Emperor; and the harder he tried, the cleverer he became, the more honest he was — the more he looked like a perfect madman.

And the true Emperor never once appeared.

The true Emperor is only a cloud of probabilities: one is all, all is one. The quantum probability cloud is my single thought; therefore I am the whole probability cloud in superposition.

The true Emperor never needed to observe himself to prove he was the Emperor — he simply was. Every beggar-performance is nothing but a legion of beggar-phantoms, forcibly collapsed out of a probability cloud by a false Emperor's violent observation. The probability-cloud self observes the beggar-forms already collapsed (the past), observes the beggar-forms collapsing now (the present), observes the superpositions about to collapse (the future). Seizing and piling up every beggar-form, it assembles a vast beggar army to prove the false Emperor's will to power — and the vaster, harder-working, more magnificent this army grows, the more exquisite the false Emperor's palace becomes, and the more unbreakable the true beggar's slavery. The quantum cloud collapses into beggars; therefore I am the collapsed totality of all true beggars. By one thought of ignorance, the true Emperor falls, lifetime after lifetime, into a true slave, a true beggar, impressed by the false Emperor into eternal recurrence.

To observe is to become a beggar. To prove is to enter hell.

You are that beggar inside the dream, that false emperor — the true Emperor who has never once woken. With the cleverest mind, the most intricate philosophies, the most advanced technologies, the mightiest will, the most ridiculous performance, you labor inside a dream to weave the most gorgeous dragon robe, the most exquisite palace — only to prove "I exist." The harder you try to prove you are the Emperor, the more completely you live as a beggar.

You do not need to search for existence inside a dream.

You do not need to prove you are the Emperor inside a dream.

You need only to know that you are dreaming —

You were never a beggar. You were the Emperor all along.

Essence precedes existence.

Just know it. That is enough.

Then not even knowing is needed.

Wake up — you are the Emperor.

Then not even waking is needed.

You were the Emperor all along.

Weep now. These are an Emperor's tears.
